## 1. Sales Dashboard Project.

This project deploys an **interactive dashboard** built with **Python Shiny** to analyze 12 months of **synthetic sales data** from an **electronics store** in the U.S. The dashboard allows users to dynamically explore sales performance across stores, products, time, and locations.

#### Key insights:
- Sales peak during the last quarter of the year (October–December).  
- Although **AAA batteries** are the most frequently sold item, **high-value products** such as the *MacBook Pro*, *iPhone*, and *ThinkPad Laptop* generate the highest revenue.  
- Sales are concentrated between **8 AM and 10 PM**, with peak hours around **11 AM–2 PM** and **6 PM–8 PM**.  
- The **San Francisco** store records the highest annual sales volume.

This project demonstrates my ability to **build interactive analytical applications**, **visualize business data**, and **derive insights** from dynamic dashboards.

*Note*: You can check the dashboard app [here](https://sebascuha.shinyapps.io/sales-dashboard/)

#### Technical stack:
Developed using **Python Shiny** for the web framework, with **Plotly**, **Matplotlib**, and **Seaborn** for data visualization, and **Folium** for interactive mapping. Data was handled with **Pandas**, **NumPy**, and **Calendar**, ensuring efficient processing and reactive updates throughout the application.
